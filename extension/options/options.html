Who presses Post

ReelForge attaches your video, fills in the text and stops. You check the page and press Post. That is how it works unless you switch on one of these.

The platforms' terms restrict automated posting, and nobody can promise an account is safe when a program presses the button. Pressing Post yourself is the safer choice.
